My experience spans taking applications from requirements and technical design through implementation, testing, release, and ongoing improvement. I specialize in Kotlin, modular architecture, asynchronous systems, and cross-platform development.

I take ownership of the pieces that connect an application: navigation, state management, data flows, persistence, networking, and platform integrations. I work with product, design, and backend teams to turn requirements into maintainable software.

Alongside production work, I create my own applications and contribute to open source. TetrisLite explores multiplatform development; Logica, now Funfolio, is a puzzle product released on Android and iOS.
